**Main artefacts:** Unhandled specifications queue; review/approve flow; approval spawns tasks.

**Human review gate:** Specifications are not implementation-ready until approved — distinct from embedded LLM drafting in [Problem](../problem/).

**After approval:** Tasks appear in [Task view](../task/); counts update on [Dashboard](../dashboard/).
