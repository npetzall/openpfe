# Specification view — requirements

## FR-UI-5 Specification view

- **FR-UI-5.1** List specifications that are **unhandled** (pending review/approval).
- **FR-UI-5.2** Open, review, **approve**, or send back for revision.
- **FR-UI-5.3** **Approval creates tasks** with user-visible confirmation.
- **FR-UI-5.4** Approved specifications distinguishable from unhandled (filter or separate list).
