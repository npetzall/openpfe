# Problem

## Summary

There is too little guidance on boundaries, ownership, and where concerns should live—so the system stays mentally and structurally "one thing" until size makes that painful.

## Longer problem

Small scopes hide the gap: without explicit rules for modules, layers, or feature seams, contributions accumulate into a single lump. Teams lack a shared map of what should be separate, coherent, and decoupled—or the map is informal and drifts.

## Open questions

- Is the missing guidance mostly undocumented, or documented but ignored under deadline pressure?
- Who “owns” architectural boundaries today—anyone, no one, or rotating ad hoc?
