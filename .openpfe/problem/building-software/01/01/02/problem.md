# Problem

## Summary

The tooling and habitual workflow make it easy to treat the work as a single undifferentiated mass—not to keep parts separate yet coherent and loosely coupled.

## Longer problem

The path of least resistance favors one big context, one tree, one flow. Very little in the environment rewards carving seams, enforcing dependency direction, or working in bounded slices. What feels fine for small efforts becomes friction at larger scale—because the stack never trained the team to operate with real boundaries.

## Open questions

- Is the pain worse from the AI context window, the monorepo layout, frameworks, or lack of automation (lint/modules/generators)?
- Would stronger physical or virtual boundaries in the repo change behavior, or would they be bypassed?
